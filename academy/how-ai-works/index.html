By the end of this course you will be able to

  • Explain how machine learning differs from traditional programming
  • Describe supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning, and how models are evaluated
  • Explain neural networks, parameters and why they're hard to interpret
  • Describe tokens, embeddings, context windows and next-token prediction
  • Explain how LLMs are pre-trained and post-trained, including reasoning models
  • Explain why LLMs hallucinate and how RAG, tools and human review help

Who it's for

Anyone who uses AI and wants to understand what's happening under the hood — managers, analysts, educators, policy and compliance professionals, and people considering a technical AI career. No maths or coding needed (one optional code example).
