By the end of this course you will be able to

  • Explain why AI output is hard to judge and how automation bias works
  • Recognise the main types of AI error and red flags
  • Fact-check AI claims and citations using SIFT and lateral reading
  • Test outputs for bias using counterfactual checks
  • Verify numbers, analyses, spreadsheet formulas and code
  • Decide when a human must decide, and apply a proportionate validation checklist

Who it's for

Anyone who relies on AI-generated content or decisions — and especially reviewers, managers, analysts, writers, researchers and anyone signing off AI-assisted work.
