Key dates at a glance

  • 1 August 2024

    The AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) enters into force.

  • 2 February 2025

    Prohibited practices and AI literacy obligations apply.

  • 2 August 2025

    General-purpose AI model obligations, governance structures and penalties apply.

  • 2 August 2026

    Article 50 transparency obligations apply. The Commission's GPAI enforcement powers begin.

  • 2 December 2026

    Deadline for watermarking by generative AI systems already on the market before 2 August 2026; new prohibitions on non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM generators fully apply.

  • 2 August 2027

    GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must comply.

  • 2 December 2027

    High-risk obligations apply to stand-alone (Annex III) systems.

  • 2 August 2028

    High-risk obligations apply to AI in regulated products (Annex I).
