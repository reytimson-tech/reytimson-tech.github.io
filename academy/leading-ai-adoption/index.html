By the end of this course you will be able to

  • Diagnose the human barriers to AI adoption in a team
  • Communicate a case for change honestly, including about jobs
  • Design AI skills programmes and champions networks that change behaviour
  • Redesign workflows with the people who do the work
  • Create clear, fair rules for AI use that build trust
  • Measure, reinforce and scale adoption over time

Who it's for

Team leaders, managers, HR and L&D professionals, transformation leads and executives responsible for introducing AI to their organisation. No technical background needed.

Built on trusted sources

Draws on Microsoft's AI business and adoption guidance, Google's generative AI leadership path, IBM SkillsBuild, AWS and Anthropic enterprise resources, and widely used change-management frameworks.
