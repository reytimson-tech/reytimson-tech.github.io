By the end of this course you will be able to

  • Explain what AI, machine learning and generative AI are, in plain English
  • Recognise the main types of AI and how each tends to fail
  • Decide which tasks to delegate to generative AI — and which not to
  • Protect personal and confidential data when using AI tools
  • Spot hallucinations, bias, deepfakes and over-reliance
  • Use AI accountably, with appropriate transparency and human oversight

Who it's for

Everyone at work — no technical background needed. Ideal as a foundation for whole teams, and as a way for organisations to support the AI literacy their staff need.
