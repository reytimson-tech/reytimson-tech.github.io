By the end of this course you will be able to

  • Explain the main ways AI creates business value — and where it doesn't
  • Discover, describe and prioritise AI use cases
  • Build a credible business case with full costs and measurable outcomes
  • Make build, buy and partner decisions and evaluate vendors
  • Design an operating model and proportionate governance
  • Plan a three-horizon roadmap and report value to leadership

Who it's for

Executives, senior managers, strategy and transformation leads, and anyone shaping how their organisation uses AI. No technical background needed — AI Literacy for Work is a helpful start.

Built on trusted sources

Draws on Microsoft's AI business training, the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework for AI, Google's generative AI leadership learning path, Anthropic and OpenAI enterprise guidance, and IBM resources. Each module links to free resources for going further. Last reviewed October 2026.
