Data Quality & Preparation for AI

    Why data quality decides AI quality

    Garbage in, garbage out; six dimensions of data quality; why AI raises the stakes; the preparation workflow; and the practice dataset.

    Profiling and exploring data

    Structural profiling, categorical value counts, numerical summaries and outliers, validity checks, visual profiling and data quality reports.

    30 min

    Cleaning data

    Logging decisions, duplicates, standardising categories, fixing types and invalid values, missing value strategies and outliers.

    35 min
  4. 4

    Labelling and annotation

    Why labels matter, labelling guidelines, Cohen's kappa in code, active learning, weak supervision and LLM-assisted labelling, finding label errors, and labellers' welfare.

    30 min
  5. 5

    Representativeness, splits and leakage

    Comparing data with the population served, proxies and historical bias, stratified, time-based and entity splits, data leakage, and leakage-safe preprocessing pipelines.

    35 min
  6. 6

    Documenting and governing data

    Datasheets, lineage and versioning, UK GDPR principles for AI data, pseudonymisation in code, the EU AI Act's Article 10, and continuous data quality checks.

    30 min

By the end of this course you will be able to

  • Assess data against six dimensions of quality
  • Profile a dataset and write a data quality report
  • Clean data with documented, defensible decisions
  • Design labelling guidelines and measure agreement
  • Check representativeness, split data correctly and prevent leakage
  • Document, protect and govern data for AI

Who it's for

Analysts, data scientists, engineers and anyone preparing data for AI or machine learning. You'll need basic Python and pandas (see Python for AI).
