Modules

  1. 1

    The machine learning workflow

    Framing problems, classification vs regression, the end-to-end workflow, scikit-learn's fit/predict pattern, the practice dataset and a first model against a baseline.

    30 min
  2. 2

    Regression: predicting numbers

    Linear regression, training and evaluating against a baseline, MAE, RMSE and R², interpreting coefficients, and inspecting errors.

    30 min
  3. 3

    Classification and evaluation

    Logistic regression and probabilities, decision trees, confusion matrices, precision, recall, F1 and ROC-AUC, choosing thresholds, and imbalanced classes.

    35 min
  4. 4

    Better models: overfitting, ensembles and validation

    Diagnosing overfitting, random forests and gradient boosting, k-fold cross-validation, hyperparameter tuning with grid search, and leakage-safe pipelines.

    35 min
  5. 5

    Unsupervised learning

    Segmentation with k-means, scaling, choosing the number of clusters, PCA for visualisation, and anomaly detection with isolation forests.

    30 min
  6. 6

    Interpreting and deploying models

    Permutation importance, error analysis by group, saving models safely, deployment and monitoring, model cards, and choosing between classic ML and LLMs.

    30 min

By the end of this course you will be able to

  • Frame a business problem as classification or regression, with a baseline
  • Train and evaluate regression models with MAE, RMSE and R²
  • Train classifiers and choose thresholds using precision, recall and AUC
  • Diagnose overfitting and compare models with cross-validation and tuning
  • Segment data with k-means and visualise it with PCA
  • Interpret models, check performance by group, and deploy and monitor them

Who it's for

Analysts, developers and aspiring data scientists who know basic Python and pandas and want practical machine learning skills. Taking Data Quality & Preparation first helps.
